Status: **P0 - open; UHS correction required**

## What Happened

- Aetna reported that no active health plan could be found after the new
  2026-2027 plan year began on August 1.
- The student health-insurance premium was charged and paid through the
  University account, but the Aetna eligibility record was not active.
- The August 5 fourth-notice enrollment link did not allow the online process
  to be completed or confirmed.
- This is an urgent medical-access problem because ongoing chronic-condition
  care, prescriptions, laboratory work, and appointments cannot safely be
  interrupted.

The exact cause is not yet confirmed. The current evidence is consistent with
an enrollment or eligibility-roster failure during the transition from the
prior plan, which ended July 31, to the new Aetna plan beginning August 1.
Payment on the student account must not be treated as proof that Aetna has
activated the member record.

## Escalation Sent

On August 5, an email titled `MEDICAL EMERGENCY: 2026-2027 Aetna coverage not
active; enrollment link failed` was sent from the UofR account to the official
UHS Insurance Advisors. Gmail confirmed `Message sent`.

A second direct escalation was sent at approximately 9:11 PM to Joyce Flagg,
Senior Student Insurance Advisor, with the central UHS Insurance Advisors and
Ashley Johnston copied. Historical UofR correspondence shows that Joyce
responded quickly and verified enrollment details during two prior insurance
problems. Ashley was copied only for student-account charge verification. RIT
and Gallagher contacts from the older RIT-plan termination dispute were not
included because they cannot activate the current UofR Aetna eligibility
record. Gmail confirmed the second message was sent.

The message requested:

1. Immediate activation effective August 1 with no coverage lapse.
2. Temporary proof of coverage or a member ID while Aetna updates.
3. Instructions for obtaining urgent prescriptions and medical care during
   the correction.
4. Written confirmation that appointments, prescriptions, and claims during
   the administrative gap will be covered.
5. Reconciliation of the paid University charge with Aetna's enrollment
   record.

The student ID and birth date requested by UHS were included in the private
email. They are deliberately omitted from Git. The current license image,
address, medical details, and account data must also remain in secure sources.
