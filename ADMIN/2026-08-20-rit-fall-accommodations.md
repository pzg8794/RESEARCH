# RIT Fall 2026 Accommodation Requests

## Source and Status

- **Source:** RIT Disability Services Office email received 17 August 2026.
- **Required action:** Request approved accommodations separately for each Fall 2026 course in the MyDSO Student Portal.
- **Status:** Not yet completed.

## Action Checklist

1. Open the [MyDSO Student Portal](https://bachelor.accessiblelearning.com/RIT/dashboard/Default.aspx).
2. Read and sign any required accommodation e-forms.
3. Open **List Requests** and select Fall 2026.
4. Select each course where accommodations will be used.
5. Choose the approved accommodations appropriate for each course.
6. Submit the requests and verify that the notification letters are issued to faculty, with Piter copied.
7. Record completion here; do not store medical documentation or diagnoses in this repository.

## Evidence Boundary

This public-safe record stores only the administrative action and completion state. Medical records, diagnoses, accommodation details, and notification letters remain private.
