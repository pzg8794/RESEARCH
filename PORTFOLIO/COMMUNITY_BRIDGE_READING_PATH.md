# Community Bridge Reading Path

Verified: 2026-08-10

This is a reader-first path through the work that best explains Piter Garcia's
commitment to neurodivergent, culturally and linguistically diverse (CLD),
chronically ill, disabled, and otherwise marginalized young people. It is meant
for trusted community partners who want to understand the person, the purpose,
and the practice behind the work.

## Core sequence

| Order | Reading | Question it answers | Artifact status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Why I Teach: Becoming the Teacher I Needed](https://github.com/pzg8794/ED400-Comprehensive_Teaching_Portfolio/blob/main/personal-statement/personal-statement.md) | **Who am I, and why does this work matter to me?** Connects Dominican-American and first-generation identity, neurodivergence, chronic illness, cultural translation, computer science, dignity, agency, and behavior as communication. | Publicly available in the teaching portfolio; formal submission status is not asserted here. |
| 2 | **What Learning Should Feel Like: Joy, Access, and Shared Possibility** | **What should young people experience when they learn?** Shows joy, access, collaboration, design, explanation, and community responsibility through concrete K-5 learning moments. | Use `02-What-Learning-Should-Feel-Like-Joy-Access-and-Shared-Possibility.pdf`, the reviewed lower-disclosure five-page derivative verified in the restricted UofR-owned community-partner collection on 2026-08-10. Do not substitute the older public-repository PDF. |
| 3 | [Teaching AI as a Learning Partner](https://github.com/pzg8794/edu442-impacting-practice-ai-pd/blob/main/submission/submission-packet.pdf) | **What can I build and contribute?** Turns inclusive values into a practical educator system that protects student voice, privacy, judgment, ownership, and independence. | Compiled public submission packet; this index does not independently establish a Blackboard receipt. |

The sequence is intentional: **identity and purpose -> the learning experience
young people deserve -> a concrete model that can scale through educators and
systems**. For a first community conversation, these three are stronger than a
large packet because each piece advances one part of the same story.

Provenance note: the Joy-Oriented Literacy source repository is
[`pzg8794/EDU498-joy_oriented_literacy_pursuit_journal`](https://github.com/pzg8794/EDU498-joy_oriented_literacy_pursuit_journal),
but its published PDF contains additional personal disclosure and placement
evidence. It is an internal discovery reference here, not the recommended
community-partner link.

## Deeper follow-up readings

These are strong second-conversation materials after the core sequence:

- [Who Gets to Name, Interpret, and Design?](https://github.com/pzg8794/EDE448/blob/main/modules/Module1/assignments/module1-journal/draft.md) examines labels, behavior as communication, family and student knowledge, and the responsibility to redesign environments before blaming learners. The linked file is marked ready to submit; it does not establish formal submission.
- [From Compliance to Communication](https://github.com/pzg8794/EDE448/blob/main/modules/Module2/assignments/prevent-teach-reinforce-planning/draft.md) demonstrates how access, agency, and richer evidence change positive-support planning. The linked draft says almost ready and not submitted, while an adjacent course README records Piter's confirmation of submission. Reconcile that status and verify completion of the official PTR form before presenting it as final.

Review the exact artifact again before external sharing. Public repository
visibility is not, by itself, evidence that every file in a broad course
workspace is appropriate for a particular audience.

## Hold for a later, intentional choice

The following works are deeply aligned but contain more personal, medical,
family, institutional, or placement context. They should not be first-contact
attachments:

- *This I Believe: Teacher Agency as Algorithmic Justice*
- *Literacy Across Languages, Bodies, and Systems*
- *Literacy as Power: Communication Access, Neurodivergence, and Social Justice in STEM Education*
- unfinished Puzzle Plan, EQUITAS, or IDAI diagnostic-justice drafts

If one becomes important for a later conversation, prepare a clean excerpt or
reviewed derivative instead of casually linking a broad workspace.

## Audience and privacy rule

- Share the direct reviewed artifact, not the whole course, placement, or
  RESEARCH repository.
- Do not share raw transcripts, child media, identifiable youth records,
  private correspondence, or internal collaboration material.
- For restricted relationships, use a University of Rochester-owned copy with
  named-viewer access rather than an anyone-with-the-link permission.
- Keep artifact completion, publication, and formal submission status distinct.
