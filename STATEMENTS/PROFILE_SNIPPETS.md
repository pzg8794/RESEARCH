# Profile Snippets

Updated: 2026-06-18

Reusable bios and posting snippets for PhD-facing profile updates.

## 3-Sentence Website or GitHub Bio

I am a machine learning researcher working on adaptive decision systems under uncertainty, with current emphasis on quantum routing and resource allocation. My research also includes computational biology and responsible AI, with a focus on methods that are rigorous, reproducible, and socially meaningful. I build across research, implementation, and education to connect technical depth with real-world impact.

## LinkedIn About (short)

I work at the intersection of machine learning, adaptive systems, and applied research problems where decisions must be made under uncertainty. My current work spans quantum routing and resource allocation, computational biology, and responsible AI, with a consistent focus on reproducibility and practical impact. I am building toward PhD research that combines technical rigor with socially meaningful outcomes in scientific and educational contexts.

## Application Form Bio (about 120-150 words)

I am an ML researcher focused on adaptive decision-making systems for high-uncertainty environments. My technical work includes quantum routing/resource allocation, fairness-aware machine learning, and computational biology projects that require strong modeling, evaluation, and reproducibility practices. In parallel, I maintain an education-facing portfolio that translates technical AI ideas into inclusive learning and teaching contexts. Across these areas, my core research interest is how to design robust ML systems that remain useful, interpretable, and equitable in real operational settings. I am preparing for PhD work centered on machine learning methods with interdisciplinary application, especially where systems-level constraints and human-centered outcomes must be jointly optimized.

## Weekly Post Skeleton

Use this structure for each weekly post.

1. Context: one sentence on the problem.
2. Update: one concrete thing completed this week.
3. Evidence: one public-safe link.
4. Why it matters: one sentence tied to your PhD direction.
5. Invitation: one question or collaboration line.

## Platform Formatting Rules

- LinkedIn: 120-220 words, one key link, one technical takeaway.
- GitHub profile README: concise changelog note plus one evidence link.
- Website/ResearchGate: 100-180 words, include methods/result framing.
- ORCID/Scholar/OpenReview: metadata updates only unless there is a formal new artifact.
