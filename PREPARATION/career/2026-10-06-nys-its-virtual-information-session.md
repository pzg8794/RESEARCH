## Session focus

The session is designed to explain:

- the types of roles available at NYS ITS;
- how the New York State hiring process works;
- tips for applying to state positions; and
- what it is like to work in the public sector.

## Preparation goals

1. Identify NYS ITS role families that align with Piter’s background in software engineering, data systems, data science, AI/ML, research, and education/technology.
2. Understand which NYS hiring pathways are most relevant for experienced professionals, graduate students, and candidates approaching graduation.
3. Clarify how civil-service requirements, examinations, eligible lists, provisional appointments, traineeships, and direct-hire pathways apply to technology roles, if discussed.
4. Learn how NYS ITS evaluates professional experience relative to degree and years-of-experience requirements.
5. Identify practical next steps and suitable job-search channels after the session.

## Questions to consider asking

- For candidates with substantial private-sector engineering and data experience who are completing graduate degrees, which NYS ITS job families or hiring pathways are usually the best entry point?
- Are there technology roles where professional experience can satisfy or substitute for particular educational requirements?
- Which positions are commonly filled through direct-hire or non-exam pathways, and which typically require a civil-service examination or eligible list?
- How should an applicant present overlapping experience in software engineering, data engineering, data science, AI/ML, research, and education when applying through the NYS system?
- Are remote or hybrid arrangements common for NYS ITS roles, and where are location expectations stated in postings?
- What are the most common mistakes strong technical candidates make when applying for NYS positions?
