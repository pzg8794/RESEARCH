# Preparation Center

Last updated: 2026-08-04

This directory is the public-safe preparation center for active applications,
recruiter calls, interviews, advising meetings, information sessions, and
scheduled administrative appointments.

## Operating Rule

Every submitted application, active recruiter screening, and scheduled meeting
must point to a preparation kit before the conversation or appointment begins.
Each kit should answer five questions:

1. What is the purpose and expected outcome?
2. What should Piter Garcia say or show from his own work?
3. What questions should he expect?
4. What should he ask or verify?
5. What must be recorded afterward?

Private messages, medical details, account balances, secure links, government
identifiers, and other sensitive information do not belong in this public
repository. A kit may link to a secure portal or a public-safe record without
copying private content.

## Before And After Every Meeting

Before:

- Keep at least one full calendar day between a new meeting request and the
  meeting. Accept a same-day request only when there is no workable alternative
  and Piter explicitly chooses it.
- For interviews and advising meetings, protect a preparation block on the day
  before and at least 45 minutes of quiet review before the meeting. Avoid
  back-to-back high-stakes obligations and preserve recovery time afterward.
- Open the linked kit at least 20 minutes before a routine conversation and at
  least 45 minutes before an interview, advising meeting, or information
  session.
- Review the short introduction, three strongest evidence points, likely
  questions, and questions to ask.
- Open only the materials needed for that meeting.
- Check the live calendar for conflicts and preserve recovery time.
- Treat private family-care and medical commitments as protected blocks, and
  do not expose their details in this public repository.

After:

- Record the date, participants or organization, outcome, next action, owner,
  and deadline in the appropriate tracker.
- Save a receipt or public-safe evidence link when one exists.
- Update or create the follow-up calendar event before closing the task.
- Do not send a duplicate response or application while a verified reply is
  pending.
