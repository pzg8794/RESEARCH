- Artificial intelligence and machine learning

## Questions To Ask

1. Is every admitted full-time Ph.D. student guaranteed funding, or are
   assistantships competitive and renewed annually?
2. What do assistantships cover: tuition, fees, health insurance, stipend, and
   summer support?
3. Which faculty are accepting Fall 2027 students in AI/ML, data science,
   medical informatics, responsible AI, or related systems research?
4. Must applicants name or contact a potential advisor before applying?
5. What are the Fall 2027 application opening date, priority deadline, final
   deadline, and funding deadline?
6. How are prior master's degrees and current thesis work evaluated?
7. Are GRE scores required, optional, or not accepted?
8. Can students collaborate across campuses or with external healthcare and
   research partners?
9. What are the residence, course-load, publication, qualifying-exam, and
   dissertation expectations?
10. What outcomes do recent graduates have in research, industry, and faculty
    roles?

## Decision Standard

Do not move New York Tech into the top application tier based only on the
session. Record the funding guarantee, active faculty fit, application dates,
and research expectations first. Compare it with RIT, UofR, NYU, and the other
fully funded targets using the same criteria.
