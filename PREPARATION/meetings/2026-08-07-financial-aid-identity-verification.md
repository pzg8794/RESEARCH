## Bring Privately

- The exact unexpired photo identification required by the appointment email
- University of Rochester student identification if requested
- The appointment email available on the phone
- Any form the office specifically asked Piter to bring
- A secure way to note the staff member, completion status, and next step

## Confirm At The Appointment

1. What exact verification requirement is being completed?
2. Is any additional document, electronic form, or signature still missing?
3. When will the verification appear as complete in the financial-aid system?
4. Does the current amended aid offer require a separate action in eServices?
5. Is any deadline affected while verification is pending?
6. Who should be contacted if the status does not update by the promised date?

## Completion Standard

Do not record the appointment as complete merely because documents were shown.
Record separately:

- identity presented;
- office accepted the verification;
- system status updated;
- amended aid reviewed;
- any aid acceptance or decline decision completed by Piter; and
- Fall bill reflects the expected aid.

## After

- Add the promised status-update date to Calendar if it is not immediate.
- Save the public-safe outcome in the RIT email/action tracker without copying
  identifiers, balances, or private documents.
- Do not accept loans or make a financial commitment without Piter's review.
