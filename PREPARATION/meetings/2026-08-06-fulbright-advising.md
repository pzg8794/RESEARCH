## Opening Summary

I am finishing master's degrees in Data Science at RIT and Teaching Computer
Science K-12 at the University of Rochester in December 2026. My current
research studies fairness-aware decision systems under incomplete or uneven
information, with applications in clinical and quantum settings. I am
interested in a Fulbright Open Study/Research Award, not an English Teaching
Assistant award. I need help choosing one feasible country, project, and host
strategy that fits my research record and the actual award requirements.

## Two Project Directions To Test

### Equitable Clinical Or Bioinformatics AI

Study how incomplete, noisy, or uneven clinical or biological evidence affects
AI decisions across groups, using fairness-aware evaluation and a host with
real health-data or computational-biology expertise.

### Reliable Quantum Decision Systems

Study learning-based routing or resource allocation under incomplete feedback
in quantum networks, using current RIT research as the foundation and a host
with quantum-network or quantum-information expertise.

Do not combine both into one application unless the advisor and a plausible
host can explain why the combination is methodologically coherent.

## Questions That Must Be Answered

1. Is Piter eligible through the University of Rochester campus process while
   also enrolled at RIT and completing both degrees in December 2026?
2. Which campus deadline applies, and what must be complete by that date?
3. Which award type is correct: Open Study/Research, graduate study, or another
   officially listed route?
4. Which countries currently permit the proposed research and match the
   citizenship, degree, language, and affiliation rules?
5. Is a host affiliation required at submission, and what must the affiliation
   letter say?
6. Which project direction is more credible from the current record?
7. How many recommendations are required, what must each address, and when
   should references be contacted?
8. What are the national deadline, campus review process, interview, and final
   submission steps?
9. Can Fulbright support a research year that strengthens later Ph.D. work, or
   would a direct funded Ph.D. application be the better route?

## Required Outcome

Leave with one of these explicit decisions:

- proceed with a named award, country, project direction, host-search plan, and
  verified dates;
- complete a specific eligibility or award check before deciding; or
- close Fulbright for this cycle and redirect effort to direct funded Ph.D.
  programs or another verified fellowship.

Do not request recommendations or begin a country-specific statement until the
award, eligibility, project, host expectation, and dates are verified.
