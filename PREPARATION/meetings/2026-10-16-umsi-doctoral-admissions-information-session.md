## Questions To Ask

1. Are Robin Brewer and Tawanna Dillahunt considering Fall 2027 PhD students,
   and how should an applicant describe fit without implying prior contact?
2. What distinguishes a strong 4-6 page statement of purpose for UMSI from a
   general computer science research statement?
3. How much methodological detail should the statement include when proposing
   human-centered AI, accessibility, and equitable-technology research?
4. Does guaranteed support include tuition, mandatory fees, year-round health
   benefits, fall and winter stipends, and exactly one summer stipend for every
   student in satisfactory standing?
5. When and how do students secure additional summer research support?
6. How flexible is adviser selection after enrollment, and can students work
   across multiple UMSI research groups or University of Michigan units?
7. What evidence best demonstrates readiness for community-engaged or
   accessibility research when the applicant's prior work spans clinical AI,
   data systems, and computer science education?
8. Are interviews part of the admissions process, and what is the expected
   decision timeline after the December 1 deadline?
9. Are there application details or common errors that are not explicit on the
   public instructions?
