# Medical Appointment Preparation Checklist

Last updated: 2026-08-05

This is intentionally a public-safe checklist. Do not add symptoms, diagnoses,
medications, laboratory values, insurance information, or private portal links
to this repository.

The separate calendar preparation block is Wednesday, August 5, 7:45-8:15 PM
EDT, immediately before the 8:15 PM appointment.

## Before The Appointment

- Complete the secure check-in early through the link in the Google Calendar
  event or Circle Medical message.
- Review the current medication list and refill needs in the secure medical
  record.
- Write a short private list of the two or three most important concerns,
  including when each started, what changed, and how it affects daily function.
- Gather recent laboratory orders or results in the secure portal if they are
  relevant.
- Decide the outcome needed from the visit: assessment, refill, laboratory
  order, referral, documentation, or follow-up plan.
- Be ready 10 minutes before the appointment with identification and insurance
  information available privately.

## Questions To Resolve

- What is the next clinical action and expected timeline?
- Which symptoms or changes require urgent care rather than waiting?
- Are new tests, medication changes, referrals, or follow-up appointments
  needed?
- What should be documented or sent after the visit?

## After The Appointment

- Record the next action and due date privately.
- Add confirmed tests, referrals, or follow-up appointments to the RIT Google
  Calendar with reminders and sufficient travel or preparation time.
- Do not copy medical details into the public RESEARCH repository.
