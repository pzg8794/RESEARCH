# Fall 2027 Ph.D. Work-Session Preparation Kit

Last updated: 2026-08-04

Use this kit for the scheduled packet audit, faculty-fit shortlist, CV and
statement review, and application-opening sessions.

## Shared Decision Standard

For every program, record:

- exact degree and department;
- official Fall 2027 application status;
- official deadline and funding deadline;
- funding guarantee, stipend, fees, insurance, and summer support;
- three or four active faculty matches;
- application requirements and missing items;
- one focused research direction rather than the entire Puzzle Plan; and
- submit, monitor, or skip decision with evidence.

## August 12 - Academic Packet Audit

Verify against official records:

- all degree titles and institutions;
- completed and expected completion dates;
- the GPA that belongs to each institution or program;
- official and unofficial transcript availability;
- test requirements and whether any score is needed;
- legal name and application name, using `Piter Garcia` in public-facing
  materials; and
- current CV, research statement, writing sample, and recommendation strategy.

Do not rewrite transcript facts from memory or use a curriculum-planning sheet
as an admissions tracker.

## August 19 - Faculty-Fit Shortlist

For each target program:

1. Read current official faculty and lab pages.
2. Keep only faculty whose recent work connects directly to one proposed
   research module.
3. Write a two-sentence fit rationale grounded in a specific project,
   publication, method, or lab direction.
4. Confirm whether the faculty member is accepting students when that
   information is available.
5. Identify one primary and one backup research direction per school.

## August 26 - CV And Research Statement Review

- Use `Piter Garcia` consistently.
- Show both current master's degrees with expected December 2026 completion.
- Define the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS at first reader need.
- Center one coherent research question: reliable and equitable decisions under
  incomplete or uneven information.
- Use clinical/bioinformatics, quantum, or inclusive-CS evidence according to
  the program rather than forcing all domains into equal space.
- Remove unsupported metrics and platform claims, but keep all source versions
  available for Piter's review.

## September 1 And Later - Open Applications

- Open the official portal only after the program confirms the Fall 2027 cycle
  is live.
- Record every field, upload, recommendation, fee, waiver, and deadline before
  entering long-form answers.
- Save drafts; do not submit until Piter reviews the entire application.
- Create a program-specific preparation kit before any faculty or admissions
  meeting.
- Schedule meetings with at least one full calendar day between the request and
  meeting, plus a preparation block and recovery buffer.
