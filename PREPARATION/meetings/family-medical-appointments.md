# Family Medical Appointment Preparation Checklist

Last updated: 2026-08-05

This is intentionally public-safe. Keep names beyond the family relationship,
symptoms, diagnoses, medications, procedure details, insurance information,
portal links, and private message content in the secure source or private
calendar only.

## Before The Appointment Or Call

1. Confirm the exact date, time, location or phone number, check-in time, and
   expected duration from the current medical source.
2. Confirm transportation, accompaniment, discharge, and driver requirements.
3. Keep identification, insurance information, medication information, and
   procedure instructions available privately.
4. Prepare a short private list of questions, recent changes, and the outcome
   that must be obtained from the appointment.
5. Check the next calendar obligation for travel and recovery time. Do not
   treat two appointments in different locations as compatible when one ends
   exactly as the other begins.

## During

- Record the staff member or department, the instructions given, what remains
  pending, and who owns the next action.
- Ask when results, arrival details, prescriptions, referrals, or follow-up
  instructions should be expected.
- Ask whom to contact if the expected update does not arrive.

## After

- Add every confirmed follow-up, test, procedure, or phone call to the RIT
  Google Calendar with reminders and a separate preparation block of at least
  30 minutes.
- Record only a public-safe status and scheduling conflict in RESEARCH.
- Keep clinical details and documents in the secure medical source.
