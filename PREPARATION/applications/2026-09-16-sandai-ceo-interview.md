## Case-study response pattern

Use this order and keep the first answer concise:

**RECOMMEND → WHY → ACT → MEASURE**

1. **Recommend:** “My recommendation would be to…”
2. **Why:** identify the main reason plus the biggest uncertainty/risk.
3. **Act:** state the first action and who owns it.
4. **Measure:** define the success threshold / evidence and when to reassess.

Then stop and let Jeremy ask for depth. The goal is to preserve strong evidence discipline while making the decision easy to hear.

## 60-second case checklist

- What decision is actually being made?
- What is known vs. assumed?
- What is the highest-risk unknown?
- What should happen first?
- Who owns that action?
- What measurable result or threshold determines whether to continue?
- When is the next update / decision gate?
