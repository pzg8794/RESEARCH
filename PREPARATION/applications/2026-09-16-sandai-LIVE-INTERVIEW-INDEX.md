## Case study rescue framework

If Jeremy gives me a case, do this before speaking:

1. **Decision:** What decision are we actually making?
2. **Known vs assumed:** What facts do I have? What am I assuming?
3. **Risk:** What is the highest-risk unknown?
4. **Recommendation:** What should we do now?
5. **Owner:** Who owns the first action?
6. **Measure:** What metric / threshold tells us whether to continue?
7. **Next gate:** When do we reassess?

### Spoken version

> “My recommendation would be ___. The main reason is ___. The biggest uncertainty is ___. I would have ___ own the first action, which is ___. I’d proceed further if ___ reaches ___, and I’d reassess at ___.”

**Then STOP.**

## If I freeze

Use one of these exact rescue lines:

> “Let me structure that around the decision first.”

> “The first thing I’d separate is what we know from what we’re assuming.”

> “My initial recommendation would be a controlled first step rather than a full deployment.”

> “The biggest risk I’d want to reduce before scaling is ___.”

> “I’d define the success threshold before implementation so we know whether to continue.”

If I need 5 seconds:

> “That’s a good case. Give me a moment to organize the decision and the main risk.”

That is completely normal in an interview.
