## After Any Response

1. Record the response and exact next action in the
   [Job Pipeline](../../APPLICATIONS/Jobs/job-pipeline.md).
2. Create or update the role's dedicated kit if an interview or meeting is
   offered.
3. Check the live calendar before proposing times.
4. Offer times with at least one full calendar day in between and protect a
   preparation and recovery buffer.
5. Add the confirmed meeting and kit link to Google Calendar.
