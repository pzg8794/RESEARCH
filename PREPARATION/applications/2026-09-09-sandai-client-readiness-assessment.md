## Expected exercise

A hypothetical nonprofit-client case study. Allow roughly 20 minutes to read and dissect the facts, then respond in writing to situations that may contain incomplete information. The assessment evaluates reasoning, prioritization, judgment, and clarity rather than a single predetermined answer.

## Fast response structure

1. **Goal:** State the client outcome and affected stakeholders.
2. **Known / unknown:** Separate established facts from missing information.
3. **Risks:** Identify privacy, equity, operational, and implementation risks.
4. **Recommendation:** Give a concrete, proportionate next step.
5. **Validation:** Explain what evidence or feedback would test the recommendation.
6. **Communication:** State how the decision and limitations would be communicated.
