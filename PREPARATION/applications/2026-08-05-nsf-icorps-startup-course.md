## Recommended Innovation Lane

resource allocation under incomplete and uneven feedback. The framework compares
bandit policies and measures utility, reliability, latency, and group-level
disparities across quantum-network and clinical simulations.

The application should begin with one customer problem rather than presenting
the entire Puzzle Plan as a product. The preferred decision order is:

1. Test whether quantum-network researchers or developers have a concrete need

2. Test whether clinical-AI researchers or health-technology teams have a
   concrete need for fairness-aware diagnostic-allocation simulation.
3. Only describe a reusable cross-domain platform after interviews establish
   that both groups share the same job and evaluation need.

## Application Answer Bank

Use these as evidence-grounded starting points for Piter's review, then update
them with the verified implementation and ownership facts.

### Innovation

I am developing a research-stage framework for evaluating learning-based resource
allocation when feedback is incomplete, delayed, noisy, or uneven. The current
work compares bandit algorithms in quantum-network routing and clinical decision
simulations and measures both overall performance and differences across groups
or network flows. The commercialization question is whether research teams need
a reproducible tool for identifying when an apparently successful policy creates
repeated disadvantage that aggregate metrics hide.

### What Has Been Built

The documented work includes a manuscript-style research framework, simulation
and evaluation design, policy comparisons, and fairness-focused metrics. Before
submission, replace this sentence with an exact inventory of the current code,
tests, data, and validated results from the canonical DSCI601 repository. Do not
describe proposed components as completed.

### Target Customer Hypothesis

The first customer hypothesis is a research or engineering team that develops
resource-allocation policies but lacks a reproducible way to test performance
under partial feedback and compare impacts across groups or network flows. The
course would be used to interview potential users, narrow the first market, and
learn whether the stronger entry point is quantum networking, clinical AI, or a
shared evaluation workflow.

### Commercial Impact Hypothesis

If the need is validated, the framework could reduce the time required to design,
compare, and audit allocation policies, while making failure modes visible before
deployment. This is a hypothesis for customer discovery, not a claim of proven
revenue, savings, or clinical benefit.

### Why Piter Garcia

I combine computer and electrical engineering, computer science, data science,
healthcare-data and machine-learning experience, quantum-computing research, and
inclusive computer science education. My research is shaped by chronic illness,
neurodivergence, and experiences of delayed or incomplete recognition, which is
why I examine who may be repeatedly missed when systems learn from uneven
evidence. I-Corps would help me test where this work solves a concrete external
problem and what should remain academic research.
