## Technical Story If The Call Goes Deeper

My current thesis asks how limited resources can be allocated fairly when a
system must learn from incomplete feedback. Bandit algorithms repeatedly
choose among possible actions, observe the result of the selected action, and
update future choices. I apply a shared evaluation framework to routing scarce
resources in quantum networks and allocating diagnostic attention in clinical
simulations. I test whether useful context improves decisions and whether a
fairness-aware decision step can reduce differences across patient groups or
network flows without sacrificing too much overall performance. The framework
also tests missing, delayed, noisy, or systematically weaker context.
