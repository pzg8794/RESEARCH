# Luce Scholars 2027-2028 Opportunity Dossier

Verified: 2026-07-28

Status: **Eligibility gate not cleared. Do not request recommendations or begin
the full application until the official eligibility questionnaire is passed.**

## Official Description

The Luce Scholars Program is a 13-month leadership-development and cultural-
immersion fellowship in Asia. It is not a PhD, research grant, or guaranteed
AI, quantum, bioinformatics, or teaching position.

The program includes:

- orientation in late June;
- two months of intensive, fully funded language training;
- one 11-month professional placement in one Asian country;
- a placement developed collaboratively around the Scholar's background,
  experience, training, and aspirations;
- community and cultural immersion; and
- cohort leadership programming through the following July.

Applicants in highly technical fields are expected to participate actively in
identifying possible host institutions. The program arranges the final
placement, and no specific field, employer, country, or project is guaranteed.

## Eligibility Requirements

All of these hard gates must be satisfied:

- U.S. citizen or permanent resident;
- under age 33 when departing for Asia;
- at least a bachelor's degree before the late-June orientation;
- not disqualified by recent residence, work, or study in Luce placement
  countries.

Current Asia-exposure rule:

- significant recent time in one placement country may prevent placement in
  that country but does not necessarily prevent applying;
- significant recent time in more than one placement country can make the
  applicant ineligible.

The program says applicants with disabilities or ongoing health conditions are
eligible, while also advising applicants to consider that medical and mental-
health access varies by placement.

## Program Expectations

Selected Scholars must:

- commit to the full 13-month program from late June through the following
  July;
- complete orientation and two months of language training;
- accept one 11-month professional placement in one country;
- participate actively in placement development, especially for technical
  fields;
- work full time at the host placement;
- live abroad and engage deeply with the host community;
- remain flexible about placement and responsibilities; and
- participate in cohort and leadership programming.

The award cannot be deferred or shortened.

## Confirmed Financial and Logistical Support

The current official pages confirm:

- fully funded intensive language training;
- a location-adjusted stipend based on cost of living and housing costs;
- assistance from The Asia Foundation with housing and settling in;
- temporary lodging during the first days in the placement country; and
- paid travel, lodging, and food for required Finalist Weekend.

The current official pages reviewed do **not** confirm the previously tracked
`$32,000-$42,000`, general airfare, insurance, or development-fund amounts.
Those claims should not be used unless Luce confirms them directly.

## Application Requirements

Deadline: **2026-09-08 at 5:00 PM ET**. Late applications and late
recommendations are not accepted.

Required:

1. Personal and background information.
2. Academic information.
3. Professional information.
4. Personal statement, maximum 500 words.
5. Five short-answer responses.
6. One- to two-minute unedited phone or laptop video.
7. Electronic signature.
8. Exactly two confidential recommendations:
   - one must be professional;
   - the second may be professional or academic.

Optional:

- supplemental-material upload.

Academic transcripts are not required.

## Selection Expectations

Reviewers look for:

- significant leadership ability;
- potential for professional accomplishment;
- intercultural competence;
- initiative and creativity;
- resilience, flexibility, and adaptability;
- maturity, humility, and strength of character;
- openness to unfamiliar perspectives and ways of living; and
- sensitivity to cultural differences.

Candidates do not need to propose a finalized placement. Openness and
readiness for an unfamiliar experience matter more than having a fixed plan.

## Fit for Piter's Goals

Potentially useful:

- a tailored placement could involve technology, science, education, public
  health, responsible data systems, or disability policy;
- the international leadership and cultural-immersion experience could support
  later teaching, research, or public-interest technology work.

Important limits:

- it is not a quantum-computing fellowship;
- it does not guarantee AI, data science, bioinformatics, or teaching work;
- it requires pausing other full-time employment or study for 13 months; and
- the under-33 rule and Asia-exposure rules must be resolved first.
