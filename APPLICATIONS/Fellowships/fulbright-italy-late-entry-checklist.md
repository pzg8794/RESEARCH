## Prospective Applicant Form Handoff

The form does not allow saving, returning, or editing after submission. It
also includes legal waivers, FERPA-related permissions, conduct questions,
optional publicity and essay-use permissions, and an electronic signature.

Prepare these before starting:

- legal name and pronouns;
- UofR and personal email;
- preferred phone number;
- graduate program, degree, completion year, and current GPA;
- undergraduate institution, degree, and GPA;
- permanent address and citizenship;
- at least one recommender's name, email, title, and organization;
- current resume PDF;
- personal decisions on every waiver and permission.

Piter must review the legal language, enter private contact and academic data,
sign, and submit. Do not store those private answers in this public repo.
