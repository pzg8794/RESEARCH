### Required materials and decision gates

- Online application, resume, unofficial transcripts from all colleges/universities, one recommendation, $100 nonrefundable fee (possible reduction for eligible financial-aid recipients).
- PiLA normally places fellows for about 12 months, most starting June–August. A placement is a full-time, in-country commitment. Partner support varies; airfare, visa costs, and international medical/evacuation insurance may be Piter's responsibility. Review actual offer terms before accepting.
- PiLA's FAQ says there is no explicit age limit but that applicants more than five years past the bachelor's degree are rarely considered. Verify degree date and ask PiLA if the applicability to Piter is unclear; do not silently assume eligibility or ineligibility.
- A finalist is not guaranteed a partner placement. An offer normally has a one-week response window.
