## Outcome

University of Rochester campus review on August 24, 2026. The application must
present Piter as a collaborative English-language and cultural-exchange
assistant whose teaching, technical, Spanish-language, and inclusive-education

## Nonnegotiable Controls

1. Use the Fulbright U.S. Student application associated with the Rochester
   email address and select University of Rochester, Spain, and English
   Teaching Assistant.
2. Enter `master's` as the degree level. Use dated reverse-chronological entries
   where the form requests lists.
3. Keep statements close to the official character limits.
4. Submit drafts through the form linked from the UofR campus timeline only.
   Do not send a PDF, Google Doc, attachment, or private drafting link to an
   adviser.
5. Register all three ETA referees and the Spanish language evaluator in the
   application. Keep their contact information and portal status outside this
   public repository.
6. Do not upload an unofficial University of Rochester transcript. Confirm the
   current-degree transcript process with the Fellowships Office.
7. Mark the application `Ready for Review` at campus checkpoints. Do not submit
   nationally until the Fulbright Program Adviser gives approval.
8. Give the Fellowships Office a draft at least two business days before any
   appointment to discuss that draft.
