Verified: 2026-07-28

## Bottom line

Fulbright is still a real option. The earlier Spain English Teaching Assistant
route was the wrong award type, not evidence that Fulbright has nothing for

The strongest current Fulbright route is an **Open Study/Research Award** built
around one defined CS-related research project and a host researcher abroad.
This is a funded 6-10 month research or graduate-study grant. It is not, by
itself, a multi-year funded PhD position.

## Best project directions

The application must choose one feasible project, not combine every interest.
The strongest evidence-backed directions are:

1. Equitable and reliable machine learning for health or biological data.
2. Evaluation of AI systems across utility, latency, reliability, and
   group-level disparities.
3. Reliable machine learning methods for quantum-computing systems.
4. Inclusive computer-science education supported by responsible AI.

Norway is the first country to test against potential hosts. Sweden and Germany
are the immediate alternatives.

## Application requirements

The [official academic application instructions](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/application-components/academic)
require:

- one country and award;
- a defined project and host-country justification;
- a 1,750-character abstract;
- a 6,000-character Statement of Grant Purpose;
- short responses on adaptability, community engagement, and impact;
- transcripts;
- a host affiliation, with a signed letter when the award requires it; and
- three confidential recommendation letters.

The national recommendation deadline is `2026-10-06`. The
[University of Rochester campus timeline](https://www.rochester.edu/college/studentfellowships/deadlines/fulbright.html)
requires a complete revised application and recommendations by `2026-08-24`
for campus endorsement, with affiliation letters due `2026-09-08`.

The UofR page explicitly instructs applicants not to use generative AI to
compose Fulbright statements. Agents may organize evidence, verify award
requirements, find hosts, build outlines, track deadlines, and review
applicant-written drafts, but should not compose the submitted statements.
