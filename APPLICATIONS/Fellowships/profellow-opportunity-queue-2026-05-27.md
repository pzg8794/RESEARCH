# ProFellow Opportunity Queue

Updated: 2026-07-23

This is the working queue for ProFellow-discovered fellowships and fully funded doctoral programs.

The intent is to keep one public-safe list of:

- what was found,
- what was bookmarked,
- what still needs official verification,
- and what should be staged next for actual applications.

## Status key

- `Prepared`: application materials already staged locally.
- `Bookmarked`: saved inside ProFellow and worth keeping in the working set.
- `Needs official verification`: promising lead, but official page must confirm date, eligibility, and submission rules.
- `Monitor`: worth revisiting later, but not active right now.
- `Paused`: relevant, but blocked by timing, fee, or missing inputs.
- `Screened out`: not a fit for the current PhD / fellowship strategy.
