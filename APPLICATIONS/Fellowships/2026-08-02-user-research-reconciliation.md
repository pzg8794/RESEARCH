## Findings Adopted

The following parts of the report strengthen the existing strategy:

- Lead with one research identity: trustworthy computational decision systems
  operating under uncertainty, resource constraints, and high-stakes
  conditions.
- Use quantum networks and equitable biomedical diagnostics as two evidence
  domains for that central methods question, not as unrelated ambitions.
- Treat inclusive CS education, advocacy, and community accountability as
  documented broader-impact and translation work.
- Keep each application focused on one research question. Do not give quantum,
  bioinformatics, AI ethics, teaching, advocacy, and policy equal weight in a
  short proposal.
- For Fulbright, establish a country-specific necessity: a named host, method,
  dataset or institutional context, research question, community-engagement
  plan, and feasible deliverable.
- Avoid another general master's degree unless it adds a specific capability
  that the current computer science, data science, and teaching degrees do not
  provide.
- Match references to the actual opportunity and research plan rather than
  sending one generic letter to unrelated programs.
