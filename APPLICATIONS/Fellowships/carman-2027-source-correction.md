# Carman 2027 Source Correction

Corrected: 2026-07-28

## Bottom Line

The Carman International Fellowship was incorrectly classified as a high-fit
data, inclusive-education, and disability-leadership opportunity.

The current official Carman page describes a general international-service
role centered on staff mentoring, program monitoring and evaluation,
volunteer and donor coordination, meetings, partner communication, and
communications or fundraising content. It also says successful applicants
have an international-development-related degree focus and previous
international-setting experience. Those qualifications are not a clean match
for Piter's documented degree path or experience.

Do not submit the Carman application or send recommendation-letter drafts
unless Mosaic first confirms that Piter's data science, computer science, and
inclusive teaching background satisfies the degree and international
experience requirements.

## What Was Mixed Together

1. **Current Carman material:** the August 1 deadline, January 2027 start,
   current responsibility list, degree expectation, prior international
   experience expectation, and three direct reference letters.
2. **Older Carman descriptions:** a 2016 ProFellow interview and older Mosaic
   fellow stories describe stipend or housing support, disability services,
   inclusive education, research, data collection, and broader program work.
   These sources describe earlier fellowship arrangements and individual
   fellows; they do not establish the current 2027 role or benefits.
3. **Luce Scholars Program:** Luce is the current opportunity with an
   individually tailored professional placement, broad field eligibility,
   intensive language training, a location-adjusted stipend, and placements
   that can include technology, education, public health, or disability
   policy. That flexible, interest-aligned model is the opportunity that most
   closely matches the earlier description of why this would advance Piter's
   goals.
