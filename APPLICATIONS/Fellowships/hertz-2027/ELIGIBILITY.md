# Eligibility — Hertz Fellowship 2027

Official criteria require every applicant to:

- be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident;
- be enrolled as a first- or second-year PhD student in Fall 2027;
- pursue a full-time, in-person PhD in applied physical/biological sciences, mathematics, or engineering at a U.S. institution;
- direct study toward major near-term societal problems.

Source: [Hertz Who Can Apply](https://www.hertzfoundation.org/hertz-fellowship/who-can-apply/)
