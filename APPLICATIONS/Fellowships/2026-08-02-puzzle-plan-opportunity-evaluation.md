## Better Programs for Other

### 1. NIH Oxford-Cambridge Scholars Program

- [Official program](https://oxcam.gpp.nih.gov/) and
  [2027 application instructions](https://oxcam.gpp.nih.gov/index.php/prospective-students/how-apply).
- **Eligibility:** U.S. citizen or permanent resident with a bachelor's degree.
- **Funding:** tuition and stipend support for the approximately four-year
  doctoral program.
- **Structure:** one biomedical dissertation, co-mentored by an NIH investigator
  and an Oxford or Cambridge investigator, with time divided between the U.S.
  and UK.
- **Deadline:** December 1, 2026 at noon ET; three recommendations due December
  3 at noon ET.
- **Puzzle Plan fit:** strongest missing route for computational biology,
  biomedical AI, health disparities, and equitable diagnostics. The program
  lists computational biology and health-disparities research. The application
  must show substantial research preparation and identify plausible mentors.
  Its published recent-admit GPA range of 3.5-4.0 makes this a serious reach,
  but the `3.9` and `4.0` current graduate records and focused thesis evidence
  justify a structured review.
- **Decision:** **Start a mentor and project screen in August.** Do not ask for
  letters until a credible two-lab project exists.

### 2. DOE Computational Science Graduate Fellowship

- [Official eligibility](https://www.krellinst.org/csgf/about-doe-csgf/eligibility-program-requirements),
  [research tracks](https://www.krellinst.org/csgf/how-apply/apply-now), and
  [benefits](https://www.krellinst.org/csgf/about-doe-csgf/benefits-opportunities).
- **Funding:** `45,000 USD` annual stipend, full tuition and required fees,
  `1,000 USD` annual professional-development allowance, and up to four years
  of support.
- **Fit:** excellent for high-performance computing, AI, computational biology,
  and quantum-information algorithms. The Math/CS track explicitly includes
  machine learning and quantum-information systems.
- **Important institution rule:** a current master's student must begin the PhD
  at a different institution. This makes a new PhD at NYU or another university
  potentially compatible, but a new PhD at RIT or UofR requires direct written
  eligibility confirmation because those are the current master's institutions.
- **Deadline:** the 2027 application date is not yet posted. The program
  normally opens in the fall and the prior cycle closed January 15.
- **Decision:** **Priority fall application for a different-institution PhD.**

### 3. DoD NDSEG Fellowship

- [Official FY2027 application](https://ndseg.sysplus.com/) and
  [eligibility rules](https://ndseg.org/eligibility).
- **Window:** **August 3-October 30, 2026 at 5:00 PM ET**.
- **Funding:** three years, full tuition and mandatory fees, `3,400 USD` monthly
  stipend, and limited health-insurance support. The official FAQ states that
  there is **no service obligation**.
- **Fit:** strong for computer and computational science, electrical
  engineering, biosciences, AI, and quantum research that can be tied honestly
  to a DoD research topic. It is not a natural home for the inclusive-teaching
  branch of the Puzzle Plan.
- **Eligibility caution:** current public wording includes applicants in the
  first two years of graduate or PhD study. Because Piter has prior master's
  study but has not started the target PhD, use the FY2027 eligibility checker
  before investing in the proposal or contacting references.
- **Decision:** **Run the eligibility checker when it opens August 3; start only
  after a pass.**

### 4. GEM PhD Engineering and Science Fellowship

- [Official program and benefits](https://www.gemfellowship.org/gem-fellowship-program/)
  and [application process](https://www.gemfellowship.org/application-process/).
- **Window:** July 1-November 13, 2026.
- **Fit:** supports PhDs in computer science, data science, electrical
  engineering, biomedical engineering, and related fields. The consortium's
  inclusion mission and employer network fit the equity and career-sustainability
  goals.
- **Conditions:** funding is usable only at participating GEM universities;
  applicants normally apply to at least three member-university STEM programs;
  employer fellows complete a paid summer internship.
- **Decision:** **Strong conditional option.** Confirm current membership and
  PhD support for the actual university shortlist before requesting letters.

### 5. DAAD RISE Professional

- [Official applicant rules](https://www.daad.de/rise/en/rise-professional/find-an-internship/what-applicants-need-to-know/).
- **Fit:** paid German-company research internships for master's/PhD students
  and selected recent graduates in computer science, engineering, biology, and
  related fields. This can be a useful post-December 2026 career bridge in AI,
  quantum, or bioinformatics.
- **Funding:** recent graduates must choose placements paying German minimum
  wage; DAAD also provides travel and insurance support.
- **Window:** standing application window October 15-November 30; verify the
  2027 portal when it opens and filter for placements accepting recent
  graduates.
- **Decision:** **Monitor for the 2027 project list.** It is a career bridge,
  not PhD funding.

## Bottom-Line Recommendation

The best strategy is not to chase every prestigious title. It is to combine:

1. **Fulbright Open Study/Research** for a near-term international research
   project;
2. **Gates Cambridge and NIH OxCam** as high-reach, fully funded international
   PhD routes;
3. **DOE CSGF, NDSEG, and GEM** as U.S. doctoral-funding routes;
4. **direct funded PhD applications** as the main admissions path; and
5. **RISE Professional** as a paid research-career bridge if needed after
   graduation.

This portfolio covers the Puzzle Plan without forcing one application to carry
every interest: biomedical equity belongs in Fulbright or NIH OxCam;
AI/quantum/HPC belongs in Gates, DOE CSGF, or NDSEG; and inclusive teaching
belongs in the broader-impact and translational components or in a dedicated
education-focused PhD application.
