## Requirement Facts

- The Spain ETA Foreign Language Evaluation is required even for an advanced or
  native Spanish speaker.
- Intermediate Spanish proficiency is required by the beginning of the grant;
  advanced Spanish is particularly useful for university ETA placements.
- The evaluator submits the form through the Fulbright Online Application.
- The University of Rochester campus deadline is September 8, 2026.
