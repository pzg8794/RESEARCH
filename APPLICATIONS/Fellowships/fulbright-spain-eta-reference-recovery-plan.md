## What Recommenders Evaluate

The official Fulbright instructions ask recommenders to address the
applicant's:

- intellectual and professional preparation for the ETA role;
- ability to teach English in a classroom abroad;
- English usage, writing, presentations, and communication;
- adaptability and ability to engage with a host community;
- readiness to represent the United States as a cultural ambassador; and
- likely academic, professional, and personal benefit from the award.

Recommendations remain confidential and must be completed directly in the
Fulbright application system. Piter cannot draft or review the submitted
responses.
