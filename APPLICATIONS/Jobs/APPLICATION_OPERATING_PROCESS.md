# Evidence-Grounded Job Application Operating Process

Last updated: 2026-08-18

## Objective

Find and pursue paid work that uses Piter's established experience while he
finishes his current graduate programs and prepares to begin doctoral study in
Fall 2027. The search is intentionally broad across four connected families:

1. Education, curriculum, program coordination, inclusive computing, and
   education technology.
2. Software, platform, data-engineering, and programming roles.
3. Data science, machine learning, AI, healthcare analytics, bioinformatics,
   and quantum-computing roles.
4. Scientist, research-engineering, and applied-research roles where Piter's
   professional and research record can credibly substitute for part of a
   conventional credential path.

## Current constraints

- Through December 2026, prioritize fully remote or Rochester-area work.
- Favor paid roles with dependable work and revenue. Do not treat tutoring or
  gig platforms with no reliable assignments as primary-income opportunities.
- Check the actual schedule, start date, hours, travel, and location before
  deciding that a role is compatible with school and teaching obligations.
- Nationwide or international relocation becomes possible after December 2026.
- Never claim that Piter is currently a Ph.D. student or candidate. The verified
  framing is: two M.S. degrees expected December 2026 and preparation to begin
  doctoral study in Fall 2027.
- Never invent a skill, publication, credential, employment date, outcome, or
  production experience.

## Fit decisions

### Strong fit

The core work matches verified experience and no hard constraint conflicts.
Prepare a tailored application and advance it to verification.

### Stretch or experience-equivalency fit

A degree, years-of-experience figure, or specialized tool exceeds the literal
record, but the work substantially matches verified professional, teaching, or
research experience. Build an honest equivalency case and apply when the form
permits it. If the requirement may be absolute, send a concise inquiry rather
than silently rejecting the opportunity.

For a Ph.D.-preferred or Ph.D.-required research role, the case may cite:

- more than ten years of professional engineering and data-systems experience;
- current AI, data-science, quantum-network, education, or healthcare research;
- completed and in-progress master's-level preparation; and
- planned Fall 2027 doctoral study.

It must not describe Piter as enrolled in, midway through, or already pursuing
a Ph.D.

### Inquiry first

Use this when remote status, compensation, classification, schedule, location,
or credential flexibility is unclear. Ask the narrow question that determines
eligibility while preparing everything else that does not depend on the answer.

### Hard close

Close only for a genuine conflict: work authorization, required licensure,
non-negotiable onsite location, incompatible schedule/travel, unacceptable or
unreliable compensation, expired listing, or a clearly mandatory capability
that cannot be represented truthfully. A preferred qualification alone is not
a hard close.

## Evidence order

Use sources in this order:

1. The live job posting and application form.
2. Canonical RESEARCH records, current resumes/CVs, work packages, course maps,
   portfolios, papers, presentations, and verified submission receipts.
3. Current Google Drive academic, employment, and project artifacts.
4. LinkedIn as a supporting inventory, reconciled against canonical evidence.
5. Email or portal records for current status, deadlines, and receipts.

When sources conflict, document the conflict and use the strongest current
source. Do not silently choose the more favorable version.

## Required workflow for every job

1. Capture the employer, exact title, posting URL/ID, deadline, work mode,
   schedule, compensation, and required/preferred qualifications.
2. **Click Save in Handshake immediately.** Record `SAVED` separately from
   `SUBMITTED`; saving a job is never evidence that an application was sent.
3. Create a Git work package under `APPLICATIONS/Jobs/work-packages/` with the
   posting facts, fit decision, risks, evidence map, application path, and next
   action.
4. Map every material claim to verified RESEARCH/Drive evidence. Search course
   and project documentation when a portfolio, paper, presentation, teaching
   artifact, or technical example could strengthen the application.
5. Select or tailor the resume for the actual role family. Create a cover
   letter, interest statement, or inquiry only when useful or required.
6. Send Viber the posting, proposed application answers, account/email,
   selected documents, evidence links, and recommended decision. Viber returns
   `APPROVE`, `MODIFY`, or `SKIP`. Continue preparing other jobs while waiting.
7. Resolve modifications from evidence. Involve Piter only for a meaningful
   factual gap, risk, personal choice, signature/attestation, recorded video,
   compensation/location tradeoff, or unusually strong opportunity.
8. Submit through the authorized path. Do not stop after opening a form or
   uploading a document.
9. Preserve the confirmation page, email, application ID, and submission date.
10. Confirm the Handshake job remains Saved so it is recognizable in future
    searches. If the application occurred outside Handshake, record that fact.
11. Update the active tracker with distinct `SAVED`, `PREPARED`, `SUBMITTED`,
    `WAITING`, `BLOCKED`, or `CLOSED` status.
12. Create a calendar follow-up containing the GitHub preparation-package link,
    application link/ID, employer/title, current status, and exact next check.
13. Commit and push only the application-specific documentation and artifacts;
    do not sweep unrelated dirty files into the commit.

## Role-specific emphasis

### Education and curriculum

Lead with K-12 computer-science preparation, K-5 placement, middle-school
science teaching, environmental-science camp, inclusive design, backward
design, UDL, AI literacy, curriculum development, and cross-cultural access.

### Programming and software/data platforms

Lead with the verified decade-plus engineering history, reliable data
workflows, systems design, data platforms, ETL, analytics automation, ML
integration, and the programming languages/tools actually documented for the
role. Do not narrow the profile to "Python-based" when the evidence supports
broader high-level-language and platform experience.

### Data science, ML, and AI

Lead with current Data Science M.S. work, production data experience, machine
learning, healthcare analytics, fairness-aware decision systems, reproducible
evaluation, and relevant domain projects. State cloud, MLOps, LLM, clinical,
or interoperability skills only at the level verified by source artifacts.

### Scientist and research roles

Lead with research questions, methods, reproducible experiments, technical
writing, papers/posters/portfolios, interdisciplinary healthcare/education/
quantum work, and the professional engineering experience supporting applied
research execution. Use the experience-equivalency rule for advanced-degree
roles; never inflate publication status or doctoral enrollment.

## Failure-prevention checklist

- Every user-opened job tab is inventoried before tabs are closed.
- Duplicate URLs/IDs are collapsed without losing the job record.
- Every Handshake lead is Saved before or while it is evaluated.
- Saved, prepared, submitted, and confirmed are never conflated.
- The live posting is reread before submission; stale summaries do not control.
- No job is rejected solely because a preferred credential is missing.
- No qualification is claimed solely because it would improve fit.
- Remote, pay, schedule, travel, and start date are explicitly checked.
- Application materials use the current verified degree and employment dates.
- Viber verifies the completed packet, not an abstract description of it.
- Submission receipts and follow-ups are linked back to the Git work package.
