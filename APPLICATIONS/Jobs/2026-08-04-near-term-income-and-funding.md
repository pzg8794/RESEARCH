## Closed Or Deprioritized

- Guidepoint consultation: client selected other experts; professional future
  consideration reply sent. No further follow-up.
- Expired HESC webinar notices: no registration action.
- General scholarship newsletters or surveys that require payment, excessive
  personal data, or sweepstakes participation: do not treat as dependable aid.
- A recurring job watch for Piter's father: not authorized and not needed.

## Safety Rules

- Never pay an application fee for a no-essay scholarship without explicit
  review and approval.
- Piter retains control of legal agreements, identity verification,
  attestations, financial-aid acceptance, and grant service commitments.
- Do not record secure-account screenshots, balances, Social Security
  information, or identity documents in Git.
- A calendar event is not proof of application or award. Preserve a receipt
  and update the queue after every completed action.
