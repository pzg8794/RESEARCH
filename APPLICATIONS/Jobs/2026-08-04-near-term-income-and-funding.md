## DoorDash Pilot Scorecard

Record both shifts with the same fields:

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Gross earnings | Starting point, not take-home profit |
| Online time and active time | Separates total availability from paid delivery time |
| Miles | Captures vehicle use and route efficiency |
| Fuel, parking, and tolls | Direct costs that reduce earnings |
| Tips and Peak Pay | Shows whether the window had unusual incentives |
| Wait time and traffic | Identifies avoidable low-value zones |
| Weather, fatigue, and safety | A profitable block is not repeatable if it worsens health or driving safety |

Repeat only the better window, and only when net earnings and health impact
justify it. The August 8 Carifest may create downtown demand and congestion;
follow the live Dasher hotspot map rather than assuming the festival itself is
the best pickup area. Recheck the thunderstorm forecast before both shifts.

## Safety And Recordkeeping

- Piter retains control of legal agreements, identity verification,
  attestations, financial-aid acceptance, grant service commitments, tax
  forms, and banking.
- Keep platform identity documents, balances, tax data, and payout credentials
  out of Git.
- A calendar block is not proof of application, work, payment, or award.
  Preserve the platform receipt and update the queue after each action.
- Use only one account per platform and accurate eligibility information.
- Never drive when illness, medication, fatigue, weather, or vehicle condition
  makes the shift unsafe.
