# Job Search Operations

This directory is the public-safe control center for employment opportunities,
applications, recruiter conversations, and follow-up work.

## Current Search Constraints

- Available now for remote work that can fit alongside graduate study.
- Remote or Rochester-area work is required through December 2026.
- Nationwide and international relocation is possible after December 2026.
- Priority fields:
  - Data science, machine learning, and AI
  - Quantum computing and research engineering
  - Bioinformatics and computational biology
  - Computer science teaching, inclusive education, and education technology

## Operating Rules

1. Record a lead before or immediately after taking action.
2. Distinguish `remote now` from `relocation after December`.
3. Tailor a resume only for an active role with a real application path.
4. Store summaries, not private message bodies or personal email addresses.
5. Record submission evidence and the next follow-up date.
6. Remove a UofR email from the starred queue only after it has been handled and
   logged.

## Status Vocabulary

- `Action now`: a current, realistic opportunity with an immediate next step.
- `Waiting`: a reply or employer action is pending.
- `Future`: relevant after December 2026 or for a later start date.
- `Closed`: unavailable, expired, declined, or clearly incompatible.
- `Needs confirmation`: blocked on a legal agreement, identity verification, or
  another decision that must be made by Piter.
