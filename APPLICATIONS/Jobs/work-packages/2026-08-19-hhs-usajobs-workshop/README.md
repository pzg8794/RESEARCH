## Why this fits

This directly supports active federal applications and improves the applicant's
ability to search USAJobs, interpret announcements, and present qualifications
in the required federal format.

## Preparation checklist

1. Open the current federal-application record and targeted federal resume.
2. Bring questions about Schedule A documentation, specialized-experience
   language, transcript requirements, and application-status follow-up.
3. Record actionable guidance in the federal application work package.
4. Do not upload new sensitive documents unless a specific application requires
   them and the destination is verified.

## Questions to bring

1. For Schedule A eligibility, what documentation must a federal application
   include at application time, and what may be provided later?
2. How should a candidate describe specialized experience when private-sector,
   research, and teaching work overlap?
3. When should completed-degree and current-degree transcripts be uploaded?
4. What status signals in USAJobs or USA Staffing require applicant follow-up?
5. How should an applicant proceed when an otherwise complete application is
   blocked only by a pending Schedule A letter?
