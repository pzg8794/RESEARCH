## Preparation checklist

1. Open the current federal-application record and targeted federal resume.
2. Bring questions about Schedule A documentation, specialized-experience
   language, transcript requirements, and application-status follow-up.
3. Record actionable guidance in the federal application work package.
4. Do not upload new sensitive documents unless a specific application requires
