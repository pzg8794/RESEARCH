## Safe-use rules

- Do not use the exact anonymous ICNP manuscript title in new public-safe RESEARCH notes.
- Do not upload the anonymous manuscript PDF itself into the public repo.
- If a larger manuscript-style sample is needed for an application, create a sanitized application-facing copy from `GA Papers/QuantumFaultTolerant/main.tex` outside the public repo with a non-identifying title.
- Keep advisor communications, reviewer notes, and raw Overleaf feedback out of public application materials.
