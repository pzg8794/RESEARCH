## Option A: BIOL550 computational biology sample

Title: Differential Expression and Computational Analysis in a Mouse Model

Abstract (draft):
This writing sample presents a computational biology workflow for analyzing high-dimensional biological data with emphasis on statistical validity and reproducibility. The project uses structured preprocessing, quality-control checks, and model-based analysis to identify meaningful expression patterns while controlling noise and technical variability. Results are interpreted through a diagnostic relevance lens, focusing on how computational findings can inform biological or clinical hypotheses. The sample demonstrates my ability to translate complex biological questions into rigorous data-science pipelines, document assumptions, and communicate outcomes clearly for interdisciplinary audiences.

## Option B: DSCI601 methods/fairness sample

Title: Data Science Methods for Reliable and Fair Predictive Modeling

Abstract (draft):
This writing sample summarizes a methods-focused data science project centered on predictive modeling under realistic data constraints. It highlights feature engineering, model selection, validation strategy, and error analysis, with additional focus on distribution shift and fairness-sensitive evaluation. The project demonstrates practical trade-off analysis between performance and reliability, and includes transparent reporting choices intended to support responsible model deployment. The sample reflects my research style: methodologically grounded, reproducible, and oriented toward high-impact applications such as diagnostics and decision support.
