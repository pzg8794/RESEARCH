Title: Fairness-Aware Bandits for Network Routing in Quantum and Clinical Settings

This writing sample is a 12-page project proposal that develops and evaluates fairness-aware bandit methods across non-contextual, contextual, and informative contextual decision settings. It connects two high-stakes domains that share partial-feedback resource-allocation structure: quantum network routing and clinical diagnostic routing. The report presents a reproducible evaluation framework, quantitative comparisons against baseline policies, and fairness-focused metrics for group-aware performance analysis. The sample demonstrates my ability to formulate technically rigorous methods, run structured experiments, and communicate interdisciplinary machine-learning results in paper-style format.
