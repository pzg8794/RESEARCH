The attached technical report presents a multi-allocator evaluation framework for quantum entanglement routing under stochastic and adversarial conditions. It compares neural and contextual bandit strategies across 60 experimental settings, focusing on robustness, generalization, and efficient resource allocation rather than single-configuration wins. The work is representative of my research because it combines quantum-systems motivation, machine learning method design, reproducible experimentation, and careful analysis of reliability tradeoffs.
