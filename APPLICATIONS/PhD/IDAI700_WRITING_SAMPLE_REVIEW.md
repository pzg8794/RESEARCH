## Decision

Use `IDAI700-Research_Paper` as the first responsible-AI packet source when a posting wants one stand-alone ethics/governance sample from the IDAI700 lane.

Keep `IDAI700-Research_Portfolio` as:

- a broader narrative companion,
- a stronger fit for lived-authority or broader-impact framing,
- or a backup when the application explicitly rewards reflective synthesis over conventional paper structure.
