# University of Washington Information Science PhD

Checked: 2026-08-03

Portfolio role: high-fit reach

Application status: opens September 1, 2026

## Official record

- [Autumn 2027 application process](https://ischool.uw.edu/programs/phd/admissions/application-process)
- [Application materials](https://ischool.uw.edu/programs/phd/admissions/application-materials)
- Deadline: December 2, 2026, at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. The application backend may display Eastern Time; the program accepts submissions through December 3 at 2:59 AM Eastern.
- Required writing: 350-word personal statement, 350-word diversity statement, and 1,000-2,000-word research statement.
- Other materials: CV, transcripts, three academic recommendations, ranked list of 3-4 faculty with a 2-3 sentence rationale for each, and an optional work sample.
- The GRE is not considered.
- Funding: at least four years of tuition, stipend, and health coverage through RA, TA, or fellowship support.

## Why it fits

UW iSchool can connect equitable AI, disability inclusion, education, information access, and sociotechnical evaluation without forcing the work into a model-accuracy-only frame. The application needs to preserve a clear computational contribution while showing why people, institutions, access, and communication are part of technical quality.

## Faculty fit to complete

Build a ranked list of four current faculty from official iSchool pages. The list should cover:

- responsible or equitable AI and data systems;
- disability, accessibility, or communication access;
- learning, education, or human-centered computing;
- health information or high-stakes sociotechnical decisions.

Do not use a generic school-fit paragraph; UW requires a short rationale for each named faculty member.

## School-specific packet

- Research statement with one central research question and 2-3 connected studies.
- Personal statement centered on the genuine health, disability, depression, and neurodivergence drivers.
- Diversity statement showing concrete teaching, access, and mentoring practice.
- Optional work sample selected for both rigor and readability.
