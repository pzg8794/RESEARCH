## Why it fits

Michigan provides the clearest national bridge among accessible computing,
human-centered artificial intelligence, educational technology, and equitable
community systems. It broadens the portfolio beyond model development while
retaining a concrete technical and evaluative contribution.

## Location and financial feasibility

- The program is based on Ann Arbor's North Campus.
- Published 2026-27 graduate housing rates include utilities. Northwood shared
  apartments cost $861-$959 per person monthly, Northwood one-bedrooms cost
  $1,277-$1,445, and Munger suites cost $1,329-$1,486.
- University transportation includes fixed routes, late-night service, and
  paratransit.
- [Student Accessibility and Accommodation Services](https://studentaccessibility.umich.edu/)
  handles academic and housing accommodations.
- [ECRT](https://ecrt.umich.edu/) provides consultation, supportive measures,
  disability-access support, and discrimination and harassment response.
- The [2025 Ann Arbor Annual Security and Fire Safety Report](https://dpss.umich.edu/reports-and-data/annual-security-and-fire-safety-report)
  contains three years of reported-crime statistics for campus, adjoining
  property, and university-controlled off-campus locations. It does not
  guarantee individual safety.

Application-stage feasibility passes because support is guaranteed and a
lower-cost university housing path is documented. Recalculate the budget from
the actual stipend, insurance terms, taxes, and housing availability before
accepting an offer.

## School-specific packet

  research area and two or three potential UMSI advisers.

- Applicable English-proficiency results; verify the exemption in the live
  application.
