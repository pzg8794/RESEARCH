## Funding record

Stanford GSE provides every admitted PhD student with five academic years of
tuition aid plus fellowship and assistantship support intended to cover the
standard single-student cost of attendance. Funding is contingent on
satisfactory academic progress and assistantship performance; no separate
funding application is required.

The published 2025-26 structure provides $56,349.60 across four quarters in
each year. Year one combines a fellowship with a 25% assistantship, years two
through four use 50% assistantships, and year five combines a 25%
assistantship with a graduation-year fellowship. Assistantship workloads are
10 or 20 hours per week. Fall 2027 rates and Cardinal Care subsidy terms will
change and require an offer-level review.

## Why it fits

LSTD supports a coherent agenda in computing and AI education, teacher
learning, technology design, and evidence-centered learning research. The
application should foreground a tractable computing-education problem rather
than present unrelated interests in AI, accessibility, and teaching.

## Location and financial feasibility

- Bay Area cost pressure is high. Stanford says the doctoral package covers
  its basic standard single-student budget, while off-campus living may cost
  10%-40% more.
- Graduate housing is described as below local-area rents and includes
  furnishings and basic utilities. Housing availability and the assigned unit
  still matter.
- The free Marguerite shuttle is wheelchair accessible and connects campus,
  Palo Alto transit, and local services.
- [Office of Accessible Education](https://oae.stanford.edu/) provides student
  accommodations. [Institutional Equity, Access & Community](https://equity.stanford.edu/)
  provides disability-access, nondiscrimination, support, and response
  services.
- The [2025 Security and Fire Safety Report](https://police.stanford.edu/security-report.html)
  provides campus crime and student-housing fire records. It does not
  guarantee individual safety.

Application-stage feasibility passes because the five-year package is
explicitly designed around Stanford's standard cost of attendance. Do not make
an enrollment decision without comparing the actual offer, health costs,
housing assignment, taxes, and relocation expenses.
