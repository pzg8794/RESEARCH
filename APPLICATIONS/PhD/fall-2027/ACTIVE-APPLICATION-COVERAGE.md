1. Every ACTIVE application must have a start/opening or recheck event and a deadline event once an official deadline exists.

4. A calendar event is never submission evidence.
5. Portal status, recommendation status, and completion require direct evidence.
6. Keep active portfolio growth deliberate: replace or explicitly expand; do not silently accumulate applications.
