The GRE is optional. GSE's school-based fee-waiver request window for regular PhD applicants closes **November 5 at noon Pacific**; this is earlier than the application deadline. LSTD applicants linked through SHIPS must apply to LSTD and another SHIPS subplan. The applicant's linked home area has not been selected in a live portal.
- **UW Information Science:** The [program materials page](https://ischool.uw.edu/programs/phd/admissions/application-materials) confirms 350-word personal and diversity statements, a 1,000–2,000-word research statement, three recommendations, and three or four preferred eligible faculty with short, specific rationales. The $90 fee is separate from submission. The [Graduate School fee-waiver policy](https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/application-fee-waivers/) requires a request at least seven days before the December 2 program deadline; the financial-need route begins only **after** the application itself is submitted. Do not pay or submit to pursue a waiver without a separately authorized submission decision.
- **Michigan Information:** The [Fall 2027 instructions](https://www.si.umich.edu/admissions-aid/apply/how-do-i-apply-phd-information) confirm a December 1, 11:59 p.m. Eastern deadline; a four-to-six-page, double-spaced statement opening with the research area and two or three potential advisers; a half-to-one-page personal history statement; CV; transcripts; and three recommendations. The GRE is not reviewed. Published fee is $75 for U.S. citizens and permanent residents, $90 for international applicants; waiver eligibility and any English-test exemption require applicant-specific verification.
