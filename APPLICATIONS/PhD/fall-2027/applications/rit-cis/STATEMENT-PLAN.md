# Statement Plan — RIT CIS PhD

## Purpose

Answer the live research-SOP criteria with a focused next-step research
identity, not a biography or a list of projects.

## Core claims to develop

1. Piter builds computational/data systems for decisions under incomplete,
   delayed, or uneven evidence.
2. QuantumMAB supplies sequential-decision and resource-allocation methods.
3. EQUITAS and the diagnostic-equity work supply measurable reliability and
   disparity questions in high-stakes settings.
4. MLADIS and software-engineering work demonstrate iterative, reproducible
   implementation rather than only conceptual interest.
5. RIT is a credible continuation only after a current faculty cluster is
   established.

## Live prompt coverage

- clear and concise thought;
- maturity and commitment to research;
- understanding of what PhD study requires;
- awareness of field advances;
- a specific research vision.

Avoid unsupported publication claims, generic AI-fairness language, and trying
to fit every education/health/quantum interest into one proposal.
