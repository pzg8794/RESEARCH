## Working research identity

The smallest coherent agenda is **reliable and equitable AI decision support when evidence is incomplete, delayed, or uneven across people and institutions**. Health and scientific-information systems provide the primary high-stakes setting. Accessibility is an evaluation requirement, not an add-on. Teaching and computing access belong primarily in the personal/diversity contribution unless a specific research question needs them.

This preserves a computational core—machine learning, uncertainty-aware decision policies, reproducible evaluation, and disparity measurement—while explaining why institutional practice and human context affect technical validity. That is the reason UW Information Science may be a stronger home than a generic CS framing.
