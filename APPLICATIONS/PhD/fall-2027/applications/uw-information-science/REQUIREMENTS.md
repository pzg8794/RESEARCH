## School-specific delta

The common dossier does not yet provide:

1. three finished statements at UW's exact lengths;
2. one or two concrete iSchool-shaped research projects;
3. a final ranked 3–4 faculty list with publication-specific rationales;
4. a verified transcript for every collegiate-credit institution meeting UW display rules;
5. a corrected academic CV using `Piter Garcia` and December 2026 completion dates;
6. an intentional decision on the optional work sample and 250-word context field;
7. an authenticated portal-field audit.
