# Fall 2027 National Funded PhD Shortlist

Updated: 2026-08-06

Status: **Active discovery and decision layer.** This file supports the
nine-program [Fall 2027 application dashboard](./README.md) and preserves the
broader comparison set. A program moves to that application board only after
its research fit, funding model, deadline, and application route are verified
from current official sources.

## Objective

Build a balanced national portfolio of doctoral programs that can support one
or more parts of Piter's research agenda:

1. computer science teaching, computing education, and learning sciences;
2. human development, disability inclusion, and accessible learning;
3. fairness-aware artificial intelligence and decision-making under
   uncertainty; and
4. quantum computing, network routing, resource allocation, reliability, and
   performance.

Financial feasibility is a selection requirement, not an afterthought. The
preferred offer provides full tuition, a multi-year stipend or assistantship,
health coverage, and a credible path to living without unfunded enrollment.

## Promotion Gates

A candidate may be promoted to the application dashboard only when all of the
following are recorded:

- an official doctoral-program and admissions page;
- at least two plausible faculty, labs, or research groups;
- an official funding statement that distinguishes guaranteed support from
  competitive or merely available support;
- the Fall 2027 deadline, or a dated recheck task when the cycle is not posted;
- application requirements and any GRE, writing-sample, or faculty-contact
  rule;
- a location assessment covering cost pressure, transportation, healthcare,
  disability access, inclusion resources, and safety evidence; and
- a realistic internal decision: `Apply`, `Conditional`, `Monitor`, or `Do not
  apply`.

## Evidence Rules

- Use official university pages for program, deadline, funding, and faculty
  claims.
- Use transparent public datasets or official campus/local reports for cost,
  safety, climate, and inclusion. Do not equate a citywide statistic with an
  individual guarantee of safety.
- Record published stipend amounts and duration only when the program states
  them. Otherwise use `amount not published`.
- Treat `funding available`, `most students funded`, and `guaranteed funding`
  as different claims.
- Do not rank a program on prestige alone. Research supervision, financial
  viability, accessibility, and daily living conditions all matter.

## Living-Conditions Audit Update

The findings below supersede the remaining-open language in the Berkeley,
Cornell, Northwestern, and Purdue living-condition cells above. They do not
clear a separate faculty, funding, or Fall 2027 cycle gate. Housing figures are
planning evidence, not guarantees of availability, and security reports do not
guarantee personal safety.

- **UC Berkeley:** projected 2026-27 [graduate housing rates](https://housing.berkeley.edu/rates-contracts-policies/rates/) run from $1,530 to $2,495 per person per month across the published graduate options. Eligible registered students have comprehensive [SHIP](https://uhs.berkeley.edu/insurance) coverage unless they receive an approved waiver; the first-year I School package includes SHIP. BayPass provides fare-free transit, the [Disabled Students' Program](https://dsp.berkeley.edu/) serves graduate students, the [Division of Equity and Inclusion](https://ei.berkeley.edu/) and OPHD provide belonging and discrimination-response resources, and Berkeley publishes a current [Annual Security and Fire Safety Report](https://ucpd.berkeley.edu/alerts-data/annual-security-fire-safety-report-uc-berkeley). **Living review complete; the two-eligible-adviser and exact-deadline gates remain open.**
- **Cornell, Ithaca:** the 2026-27 [graduate cost of attendance](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students) budgets $36,144 in non-billed expenses, including $19,962 for housing. That is close to Cornell's $36,684 nine-month assistantship minimum, so summer support and actual housing choice remain material even though Information Science states academic-year tuition, stipend, and health coverage plus summer funding pathways. Registered doctoral students receive no-cost [TCAT OmniRide](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-omniride-passes); Cornell also provides [Student Disability Services](https://sds.cornell.edu/), campus inclusion resources, and a current [Ithaca Annual Security Report](https://publicsafety.cornell.edu/clery/annual-security-report) covering three calendar years. **Living review complete; the Fall 2027 cycle gate remains open.**
- **Northwestern, Evanston:** published 2026-27 [graduate housing](https://www.northwestern.edu/living/graduate-housing/) totals range from $14,356 to $26,640 per person by unit, with utilities, wifi, cable, and laundry included. Northwestern provides campus [shuttles](https://www.northwestern.edu/transportation-parking/shuttles/routes/), comprehensive insurance through [NU-SHIP](https://www.northwestern.edu/student-insurance/) unless eligible coverage is waived, academic and housing accommodations through [AccessibleNU](https://www.northwestern.edu/accessiblenu/), [Campus Inclusion and Community](https://www.northwestern.edu/inclusion/) resources, and current Clery reporting. **Living review complete; program-level funding and Fall 2027 cycle gates remain open.**
- **Purdue, West Lafayette:** official sources verify [fare-free campus transit](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/2025/Q2/trustees-approve-3-year-contract-with-sp-plus-for-free-expanded-campus-transit-services/), a [2026-27 health-plan route](https://www.purdue.edu/hr/Benefits/gradstaff/benefits-enrollment/) for eligible graduate staff, [Disability Resource Center](https://www.purdue.edu/drc/) accommodations, [PUSH](https://www.purdue.edu/push/) and [CAPS](https://www.purdue.edu/caps/) health services, [cultural-center resources](https://www.purdue.edu/odos/cultural-centers/index.php), and the September 30, 2025 West Lafayette [Annual Security Report](https://www.purdue.edu/ehps/police/images/reports/west-lafayette.pdf). Purdue's [cost page](https://www.purdue.edu/dfa/cost/) explains its housing, food, transportation, and miscellaneous allowances but does not publish a clean graduate-specific West Lafayette living-cost figure in the verified material. **Living-cost, guaranteed-funding, and Fall 2027 cycle gates remain open; decision stays `Monitor`.**

## Portfolio Decision

Cap the active Fall 2027 portfolio at **nine applications**. Promote Michigan
Information and Stanford GSE LSTD alongside the seven already launched. This
adds one accessibility and equitable-technology route and one computing-
education and learning-sciences route without diluting the school-specific
writing effort.

Use Cornell Information Science as the first alternate after its Fall 2027
cycle is verified. Use Northwestern Learning
Sciences as the second alternate only after program-level funding and the Fall
2027 deadline are confirmed. Berkeley remains conditional because it lacks two
verified eligible advisers, and Purdue remains a monitor because funding is
competitive rather than guaranteed. Replace a failed active candidate with an
alternate instead of exceeding the cap unless the portfolio is explicitly
reopened.
