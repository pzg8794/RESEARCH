## Current research direction

Piter studies decision-making under uncertainty: how artificial-intelligence systems can allocate scarce attention, diagnostic resources, and computational capacity when evidence is incomplete, delayed, noisy, or uneven across groups. The work joins three tracks:

1. Fairness-aware clinical and bioinformatics systems, including diagnostic equity and missing evidence.
2. Reliable quantum-computing workflows, including bandit-based routing, qubit allocation, and reproducible evaluation.
3. Inclusive computer science education, disability access, communication, and learner agency.

### Current thesis

**Fairness-Aware Bandits for Network Routing in Quantum and Clinical Settings**

The thesis compares bandit algorithms that repeatedly choose among possible actions, observe the result, and use that feedback to improve later choices. A shared simulation and evaluation framework is applied to quantum-network routing and clinical decision settings. It tests whether useful context improves decisions and whether adjusting decisions to account for fairness can reduce differences across patient groups or network flows while preserving useful performance. Evaluation includes utility, reliability, latency, and group-level disparities, including cases where context is missing, delayed, noisy, or systematically weaker.

### Puzzle Plan

The Puzzle Plan is Piter's interdisciplinary framework for connecting engineering, artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, disability inclusion, lived experience, and computer science education. It treats each field as part of a larger problem: people and systems are often misunderstood when evidence is fragmented or evaluated in isolation.

### EQUITAS

EQUITAS stands for **Equity-Quantified Integrated Trauma-Aware Systems**. It is the fairness-aware framework within the broader Puzzle Plan for evaluating decisions made under incomplete or uneven evidence. Its purpose is to identify who may be repeatedly missed, quantify unequal outcomes, and design earlier and more responsive support.
